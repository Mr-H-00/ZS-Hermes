# Cherry Studio Community Edition resources

The painting template catalog, localized prompts, and WebP previews in
`painting-templates/` come from Cherry Studio Community Edition 2.2.2,
`resources/data/painting-templates/`. Translation upload icons are also from
the Community Edition. These resources retain their upstream AGPL-3.0 license.

The Qwen model icon comes from `packages/ui/icons/models/light/qwen.svg` in
the same upstream release.
